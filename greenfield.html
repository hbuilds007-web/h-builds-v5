What We Do

Our Landscaping Services

Every garden is different. We offer a complete range of landscaping and maintenance services tailored to your space and your style.

✏️

Garden Design

Bespoke garden design from concept to planting plan. We work to your brief, your budget, and your lifestyle — whether it's a low-maintenance haven or a show garden.

🌿

Lawn Care & Turfing

New lawn installation, scarification, aeration, overseeding, and seasonal treatment programmes. Healthy lawns that stay green through summer and autumn.

🪵

Decking & Paving

Composite and hardwood decking, natural stone and block paving, patios and pathways. Beautifully installed and built to withstand the British weather.

🌸

Planting Schemes

Seasonal and perennial planting designed for year-round interest. Native species, cottage gardens, contemporary schemes — we'll match your aesthetic perfectly.

💧

Irrigation Systems

Automated irrigation to keep your garden thriving with minimum effort. Smart systems, drip lines, and pop-up sprinklers professionally installed and programmed.

🗓️

Maintenance Contracts

Monthly and seasonal maintenance packages to keep your garden looking its best all year round. Fortnightly or monthly visits — we fit around you.
