What We Do

Roofing Services

From a single tile to a full roof replacement — we handle every roofing job with the same care and attention to detail.

🏠

Roof Repairs

Slipped tiles, cracked flashings, felt repairs, and ridge work. We identify and fix the root cause — not just the symptom — to stop leaks permanently.

🔨

Full Roof Replacement

Full strip and re-roof using quality materials with manufacturer warranties. New felt, battens, and tiles, done to the highest standard.

📐

Flat Roofing

EPDM rubber, GRP fibreglass, and felt flat roofing systems. Garage roofs, extensions, and commercial flat roofs installed and repaired.

Fascias & Soffits

UPVC fascia and soffit replacement — white, black, or woodgrain finish. Includes guttering, downpipes, and ventilation as needed.

Repointing, lead flashing replacement, stack repairs, and full chimney removal. CHAS accredited for chimney works.

Gutter replacement, cleaning, and realignment. Aluminium seamless guttering also available for a premium, maintenance-free solution.
