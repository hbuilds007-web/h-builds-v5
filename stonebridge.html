What We Build

From single-storey extensions to full renovations — we manage the entire project, start to finish, with no subcontractor surprises.

Home Extensions

Single and double-storey extensions designed to blend seamlessly with your existing property. Planning, build, and finish all handled in-house.

Loft Conversions

Dormers, hip-to-gables, and Velux conversions. We maximise the usable space and handle all building regulations sign-off.

Kitchen Fitting

Full kitchen installations from unit assembly to worktops, splashbacks, and appliance plumbing. Trade pricing passed on to clients.

New builds, garden walls, chimney repairs, and repointing work. Matched brickwork for extensions and repairs that look original.

Groundworks & Driveways

Foundations, drainage, block paving, and concrete driveways. Groundworks carried out with our own plant and operators.
